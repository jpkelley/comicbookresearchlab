THE OPPORTUNITY ## Scientific insight beyond appearance

Comic books began as inexpensive entertainment. Today, they are treasured memories, cultural artifacts, and valuable collectibles. Their visible condition tells only part of their story: paper chemistry, material strength, environmental exposure, and ongoing deterioration can require closer scientific investigation.

CBRL will complement professional grading with measurements of material condition, comparisons with similar comics, and evidence about change through time—helping owners make informed decisions about the objects they value.

From measurement to evidence

CBRL will connect calibrated imaging and laboratory assays with controlled experiments and repeated measurements. AI-assisted analysis will extract image features for validation against laboratory measurements. Causal models will guide investigations of how exposures and treatments affect comic-book materials.

The research program distinguishes description, prediction, and causal inference: what we can measure, forecast, and attribute to an exposure or intervention. Comparisons and forecasts will include uncertainty and remain within the materials and conditions supported by validation.

Chemistry · Color & spectra · Surface · UV fluorescence · Paper structure · Mechanical integrity · Storage environment

Questions driving our research

  • How do humidity and temperature interact to affect deterioration?
  • Can pressing or chemical brightening alter material properties while improving appearance?
  • Why do historical paper stocks respond differently to the same exposures?
  • How could alternative storage or treatment choices affect future condition?

~1,500 Founder-owned research comics

295 Issues designated for destructive research

25 years Of scientific research experience

CBRL begins with a curated collection spanning 1940–1994, dedicated workspace, laboratory and calibrated photography equipment, AI computing infrastructure, and founder labor. Designated research specimens support destructive testing to validate non-destructive and minimally invasive assessment methods.

A reference dataset that grows with every comic

Standardized assessments, laboratory validation, repeated measurements, and controlled experiments will build the CBRL Reference Dataset. It will connect material measurements with publication period, storage, treatments, and change through time. Each study will strengthen comparative benchmarks and better-tested models, creating a cumulative research advantage. A planned Research Donation and Contributor Program will help expand the specimens available for testing.

THE FOUNDER ## Meet the scientist behind CBRL

Patrick Kelley, Ph.D., is a research scientist, laboratory director, quantitative ecologist, and longtime comic collector with approximately 25 years of scientific experience. His research programs have received more than $6 million in cumulative support, and his work has appeared in journals including Science and Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.

With a Ph.D. from the University of California, Davis, and an A.B. from Harvard University, Kelley brings expertise in experimental science, instrumentation, artificial intelligence, and causal inference. His early training at Harvard’s Straus Center for Conservation and Technical Studies included hands-on scientific examination of cultural materials. CBRL connects that experience with decades of research and firsthand familiarity with comic collecting.
